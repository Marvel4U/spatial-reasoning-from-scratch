Notes:

- 3DBAG (CC BY 4.0) requires attribution: *3DBAG by the 3D geoinformation group, TU Delft*. The sunshine layers are derived from it and carry the same licence.
- OpenStreetMap data is ODbL: attribution *© OpenStreetMap contributors*. The evaluation bundle of the GitHub release (`eval_bundle_v0.2.tar.gz`) includes the 1 m label stacks of De Pijp and Zuid + Centrum (`district_labels_1m.npz`), whose establishments plane is derived from OpenStreetMap points (together with Overture places, CDLA-Permissive 2.0); these label stacks are released under ODbL 1.0, attribution *© OpenStreetMap contributors*, and ODbL share-alike applies to that bundle. Its other planes keep the licences in the table above; the bundle's `MANIFEST.md` lists them per plane.
- GTFS via ovapi.nl carries no SPDX licence at the distribution endpoint; the upstream is the Dutch national access point (NDOV) under EU regulation 2017/1926.
- Behavioural datasets surveyed for later rungs (bike counts, bike-share trips, footfall, crash records) were downloaded during the survey but never used by any model in this repository and are not redistributed; one of them (Fietstelweek) is non-commercial.
- The second district used from the search rung on (Zuid + Centrum, Amsterdam) is built by the same pipeline from a subset of the same sources under the same licences: 3DBAG buildings, BGT streets, the Amsterdam noise map (road, plus its rail and industry tables under the same terms of use), OpenStreetMap and Overture places. Its manifest is written like De Pijp's.
- The maze districts used to test the search (`spatial_data/maze_world.py`) are synthetic: generated by code from a seed, no external data.
