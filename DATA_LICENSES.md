Notes:

- 3DBAG (CC BY 4.0) requires attribution: *3DBAG by the 3D geoinformation group, TU Delft*. The sunshine layers are derived from it and carry the same licence.
- OpenStreetMap data is ODbL: attribution *© OpenStreetMap contributors*; a published database derived from it would be subject to share-alike, which is why crops containing OSM-derived planes are regenerated by the pipeline rather than redistributed here.
- GTFS via ovapi.nl carries no SPDX licence at the distribution endpoint; the upstream is the Dutch national access point (NDOV) under EU regulation 2017/1926.
- Behavioural datasets surveyed for later rungs (bike counts, bike-share trips, footfall, crash records) were downloaded during the survey but never used by any model in this repository and are not redistributed; one of them (Fietstelweek) is non-commercial.
