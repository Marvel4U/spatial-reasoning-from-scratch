## Where the gap is

Nothing I found does all of the following at once:

1. **Map image as the *input* modality + geo API tools + trained (not prompted) small model.** Thinking with Map and SpotAgent train small VLMs with GRPO on map tools, but the map is a *tool output* for photo geolocalization; the question is never "read this map". MapAgent and Spatial-Agent answer map questions with tools but only *prompt* frontier LLMs. MapReason-OSM, CartoMapQA, MapVerse, FRIEDA evaluate map reading but give the model **no tools** and train nothing.
2. **Synthetic, procedurally generated map worlds with programmatic ground truth used for *training*, not just evaluation.** MapTrace (path tracing) and Eyes Will Shut come closest but are single-task and SFT-only / non-QA. MapReason-OSM renders OSM with graph-verifiable answers but is an eval set; nobody uses such a generator as an RLVR environment with a curriculum.
3. **Tools that combine visual actions (zoom/crop) with data-layer queries (elevation, opening hours, transit) on the *same* map.** DeepEyes/PERIA have zoom-style tools, MapEval-API/GeoNatureAgent have data APIs; no work trains a model to interleave both and verifies the answer against the underlying world state.
4. **Honest small-model comparison against MapEval-API/-Visual and MapReason-OSM.** MapEval reports open models far behind; MapAgent improves via scaffolding; no paper reports a 3–8B model *trained* for this and evaluated on those public splits. That is a clean, defensible claim slot — even a negative result there is publishable-grade for a portfolio.
5. **Reward design specifics** (continuous distance rewards à la Smooth Operator, programmatic spatial assertions à la Pigeon, trajectory replay validation à la OpenEarthAgent) exist in isolation; combining them on map QA is unexplored.

Caveats: I could not verify code/weight release for Thinking with Map, PERIA, SpotAgent, MapTrace, OsmT, or PlanGPT-VL, nor MapEval's dataset license. PERIA (Jun 2026) and MapReason-OSM (Jun 2026) are the two items I would read in full before finalising DESIGN.md, since they narrow the gap most.
